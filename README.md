# Consistent

A calm, local-first tracker for the things that compound — your lifelong goals, daily habits, plans, and the days you actually show up.

Consistent is built around one idea: small actions, tracked honestly over time, add up to real outcomes.

## ▶︎ Try it live

### **[consistent-five.vercel.app](https://consistent-five.vercel.app/)**

No install, no setup — open the link and start tracking.

## Features

- **Dashboard** — Everything on one screen: a week/month/year recap, your weight trend, lifelong goals, today's tasks, a GitHub-style consistency heatmap, the week planner, and a time-management ring. Click any card title to jump straight to that section, or click a day on the heatmap to view it.
- **Goals** — Lifelong and short-term in one place:
  - *Lifelong* — a nested goal tree (as deep as you like) with progress that rolls up from its sub-goals, plus books, habits, and checklists.
  - *Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly* — simple checklists for each horizon, archived as time passes.
- **Planner** — Plan your day, week, and month — drag recurring goals onto days, add one-off todos, so you always know what's next.
- **Consistency** — A heatmap of every day you showed up so streaks are obvious at a glance, plus weight logging with trend and goal projection.
- **Time Management** — See how much of your day is already spoken for and how much is genuinely free, sized from your Life Essentials.
- **Settings** — Dark / light theme, reminders, and one-click export/import of all your data.

## Your data, your device

Consistent is local-first: everything works in your browser with no account at all. From **Settings** you can export all your data to a single file and import it back anytime, so you always own your backups. Sign in (optional) to sync the same data across devices.
