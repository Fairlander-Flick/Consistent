# Consistent

A calm, local-first tracker for the things that compound — your lifelong goals, daily habits, plans, and the days you actually show up.

Consistent is built around one idea: small actions, tracked honestly over time, add up to real outcomes.

## ▶︎ Use it now

**[consistent-five.vercel.app](https://consistent-five.vercel.app/)**

No install, no setup — open the link and start tracking. Your data stays in your own browser.

## Features

- **Dashboard** — Everything on one screen: a week recap, your weight trend, lifelong goals, today's tasks, a consistency heatmap, the week planner, and today's journal. Click any card title to jump straight to that section.
- **Goals** — Five views in one place:
  - *Lifelong* — a nested goal tree (as deep as you like) with progress that rolls up from its sub-goals.
  - *Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly* — simple checklists for each horizon.
- **Planner** — Plan your day, week, and month so you always know what's next.
- **Consistency** — A heatmap of every day you showed up, so streaks are obvious at a glance.
- **Journal** — A short daily entry: mood, sleep, and a free note, with wellbeing insights over time.
- **Settings** — Profile, dark/light theme, and one-click export/import of all your data.

## Your data, your device

Everything lives in your browser — no account required. From **Settings** you can export all your data to a single file and import it back anytime, so you're always in control of your own backups. Optional sign-in syncs the same data across devices.
